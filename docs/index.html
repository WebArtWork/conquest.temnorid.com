About the game

Command troops, plan sieges, attack enemy villages and cities, and defend your own lands. The outcome of each battle changes the political map of Temnorid.

Graphics are simple placeholder shapes for now. The game is built to be played first and polished later.

How to play

The first playable is a single village assault led by you: one Lord, fighting in person, against guards and an enemy Lord.

  1. Start outside the village as a Lord with a sword and one special strike, the Charge.
  2. Fight your way in. Guards spot you, chase you and attack; you command only your own Lord.
  3. The enemy Lord defends the village. He is tougher than the guards.
  4. You win in either of two ways: defeat the enemy Lord, or get into the hall at the heart of the village.
  5. If you fall, you see the defeat screen and can retry at once.

You win

Defeat the enemy Lord or enter the hall.

You lose

Your health reaches zero. Retry restarts the assault.

Playing with others

Up to four people can storm the same village together. On the first screen choose Play with others, host a game (friends on the same network type the address it shows, or use a short code when hosting online), press Ready and the host starts. You fight the same guards and the same Lord, and any of you can take the village: defeat the Lord or get one hero into the hall. A hero who falls comes back at the gate after a few seconds; the assault is lost only when every hero is down at once. If the host leaves, the game ends for everybody.

Updates

When a newer release is published on GitHub, the Windows game downloads and installs it by itself the next time it starts, and starts again. Turn it off in Profile if you prefer. On Android and iOS the update comes from the store or the release page.

Phones

Android and iOS builds come from the same project. On a phone or tablet the screen has a move stick (left thumb), Attack, Charge, Jump and Run buttons (right thumb), and dragging anywhere on the right side looks around. The bar at the bottom switches between the assault and the Objectives panel.
